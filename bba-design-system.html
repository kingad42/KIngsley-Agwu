Overview

A directory that reads like intelligence, not a listicle

The brief

Africa's startup ecosystem is scattered across press releases, PDFs, and word of mouth. Business Bee Africa needed to feel like a trustworthy intelligence product — the kind investors and journalists would actually cite — while staying warm and distinctly African rather than defaulting to a generic blue SaaS dashboard look.

1

Editorial over dashboard. A bold Playfair Display headline and warm cream base give the homepage the authority of a published report, not a startup landing page template.

2

The hive as structure. Every content type — Startups, Investors, Founders, Insight, Resources — is framed as one cell in the same hive, tying a wide product into a single visual system.

3

Scale, stated plainly. A dark stats strip (5,000+ startups, 54 countries, $5B+ funding) sits directly beneath the hero, so credibility is established before a single card is scrolled to.
