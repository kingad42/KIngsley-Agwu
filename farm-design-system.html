Overview

Warmth and provenance, not another grocery template

The brief

Most farm-to-table sites default to either a sterile e-commerce look or an overly rustic "barnyard" cliché. One Acre Farm needed to feel like a real farmers market: honest, seasonal, and personal — while still functioning as a clean, conversion-ready storefront for both shoppers and the farmers supplying it.

1

Editorial warmth over grocery-store cold. A deep forest green on a warm cream background reads as natural and appetising rather than clinical.

2

Two audiences, one story. The page speaks to shoppers (freshness, delivery) and farmers (fair pricing, community) without splitting into two disconnected experiences.

3

Proof over promises. A trust bar of concrete numbers — 500+ farmers, 12k+ families — sits right under the hero, before any sales copy.

Typography

Display — Playfair Display 900
Nurtured by nature, made for you
Used for H1/H2 headlines only — a serif with editorial, farmers-market warmth that contrasts deliberately against the clean sans-serif body.
Body — DM Sans
Fresh, local veggies — carefully cultivated, naturally nourishing, empowering farmers. Every vegetable is grown without harmful chemicals, using time-honoured techniques that respect the soil and the seasons.
Used for paragraphs, nav, and buttons at 14–16px — keeps the page legible and modern despite the serif headlines.
