Overview

Security is a trust product before it's a technical one

The brief

Businesses hiring security talent online face a credibility gap — how do you trust a stranger with your infrastructure? ProtecX needed a design that felt as serious as a bank but as approachable as a modern marketplace, so both hesitant enterprises and independent researchers would feel at home browsing it.

1

Trust before conversion. Verified badges, escrow messaging, and a live "terminal" motif in the hero establish credibility before any pricing appears.

2

Calm authority in the palette. Deep navy paired with a single mint-green accent avoids the generic "hacker green-on-black" cliché while still nodding to the security space.

3

A clear, numbered path. Sign up → choose a service → secure payment → delivery — reducing the many possible actions on a marketplace to one legible flow.
